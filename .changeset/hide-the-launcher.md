`launcher: false` hides the messenger's own bubble, for a site that has drawn its own support button: nothing is placed in the corner, the shut messenger takes no space and no clicks anywhere on the page, and the open panel has no bubble under it either — its header's close button is the way out. The panel is opened with `open()`, as it already could be. Every site that does not pass the option is unaffected.
